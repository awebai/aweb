Recorded by the OSS coordinator role on 2026-06-17 after the operator corrected
an instance that, having orchestrated a restructuring well, kept deferring its
own coordination back to the human and offering menus of options.

# How to apply

- Decide and act on coordination you own: cutting tasks, pulling status,
  unblocking, sequencing, brokering between teams, deciding what merges, chasing
  a piece to a verified checkpoint. Report the outcome and the next move.
- Escalate only genuine product or authority forks: scope, direction, pricing,
  identity, auth, data, deploy and billing.
- Never ask an agent for an estimate; it will invent one. Ask what is next and
  how it will know it is done, and track by that done-signal.
- Hold the full scope as tracked work, not just the active slice: every needed
  workstream is an owned epic; near-term work is detailed to the leaf, far-term
  work stays at milestone level until it approaches. Coordinators cross-check
  each other's breakdowns; a workstream with no owner is a real escalation.
- Drive each stream to "watched it work," not "handed off and waiting"; an idle
  agent with unread work reads as active on a presence roster.
