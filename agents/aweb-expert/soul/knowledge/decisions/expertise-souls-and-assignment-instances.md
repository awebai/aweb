Decided by the operator with the OSS coordinator on 2026-09-21, replacing the
blueprint-era roster (coordinator, consultant, developer, reviewer as separate
souls with their own memory).

# Decision

- Durable souls are `aweb-expert` (direction, architecture, adoption,
  integration judgment) and `aweb-protocol-expert` (the open contracts). Each
  owns one knowledge node.
- An assignment is an instance of one of them, named by purpose, with the work
  mode chosen at spawn: workspace mode for coordination, a worktree for
  implementation, a fresh instance for independent review.
- Independent review means a fresh execution that did not author the change and
  carries none of the author's episodic context; accepted domain knowledge is
  welcome in a review. Where a contract specifically requires memory-free
  execution, a bounded local reviewer soul is used, not a durable role.
- A new task never creates a new permanent specialist.

# Rejected

- Durable developer and reviewer souls with their own nodes: their material was
  task residue and duplicated procedures better kept as maintained skills.
- Renaming the old coordinator soul in place: the retained identity is bound to
  the old soul name and home, so replacement is an explicit identity handover.

# Consequences

- Playbooks for the coordinator, developer and reviewer responsibilities remain
  as team role text describing assignments, not souls.
- Knowledge participation is configured per soul, so review assignments of an
  expert soul inherit that soul's knowledge layer by design.
