# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any other project space, is expected to be respectful and constructive.

- Be welcoming to newcomers and to people with different experience.
- Focus on the work: critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that do not
follow this, and may ask people who repeatedly ignore it to leave.

To report a problem, email hello@aweb.ai. Reports are handled privately.

This is a condensed statement in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
