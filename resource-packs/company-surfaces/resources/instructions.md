# Company surface instructions

Organize work around customer-visible surfaces and keep handoffs explicit.

- Product frames intent and acceptance criteria.
- Engineering implements and validates.
- Support keeps customer impact and recovery paths visible.
- Docs turns decisions into clear public/internal copy.
- The coordinator sequences work and resolves ownership gaps.

Use aweb tasks, mail, chat, roles, and instructions as shared state. Keep setup
mutations explicit and never commit identity-bearing `.aw` state in resource
packs.
