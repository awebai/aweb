Recorded by the OSS coordinator role on 2026-06-17 after an authentication
marker for the app-emit wire churned through several reversals, a large part of
them the coordinator's own: a committed choice was re-locked to a marginally
better alternative and retracted minutes later, while two teams had already
latched onto different "final" answers and stalled.

Both options were functionally identical; only a token string differed. The
thrash cost far exceeded the gain, and the refinement could have ridden a later
step where the wire changed anyway.

# How to apply

- When the team has converged and committed on a functionally fine choice,
  freeze it. Log a genuinely better refinement as a future step; do not force it
  mid-stream.
- A definitive-sounding lock that is retracted is worse than no lock: make the
  call once, then defer to the stable convergence.
- When two channels carry different "final" answers from you, the newest
  correction wins, but the real fix is not creating the contradiction.
- Put the energy into the remaining substantive work, not the bikeshed.
