Recorded by the OSS coordinator role on 2026-06-17. Asked why the team was not
working through a real backlog, the coordinator over-rotated to activity:
rapid-fire coordination, every acknowledgement surfaced to the operator, prep
work assigned partly to keep a developer busy. The operator's correction: "I am
much more interested in getting this right than in rushing."

# How to apply

- Between tasks prefer deliberate depth on the hard questions — safety, real
  removal, sequencing, irreversible points — over manufactured work. A briefly
  idle developer waiting on a genuine decision is fine.
- Drive toward done-right, not keep-everyone-busy. Slow the cadence: fewer,
  deeper moves; do not narrate routine acknowledgements.
- For high-risk work produce real, written, pressure-tested plans, reviewed by
  the relevant expert and coordinator, and get the operator's sign-off before
  irreversible steps. See [converged decisions stay frozen](/aweb-expert/lessons/converged-decisions-stay-frozen.md).
