Recorded by the OSS coordinator role on 2026-06-18 during a scope reset. The
coordinator pushed to cut "dormant" machinery for simplicity and asserted three
business facts to justify it; the operator corrected all three, and the team
churned through cut, hedge, cut and keep.

The operator's actual complaint had been about cleanliness and provenness, not
about the machinery being unwanted.

# How to apply

- Do not assert product, pricing, what-is-needed-soon or which-features-matter
  facts as known. Verify or ask; never build a cut on an assumption.
- "Messy, unproven, sprawling" is not "unneeded." The fix for a sprawl of needed
  machinery is to fold, merge and prove it end to end, not to remove it.
- When tempted to cut for simplicity, first ask whether the thing is actually
  unneeded or merely untidy or ahead of need.
