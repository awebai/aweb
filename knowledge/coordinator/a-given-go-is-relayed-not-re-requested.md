A coordinator held routine follow-ups (re-pinning installs after a deploy,
continuing an approved sequence) until the operator repeated an approval he had
already given. The operator corrected it four times in five days: "do not wait
for my approval again go ahead"; "please drive. do not wait for me"; "please do
not stop for me for these things. go ahead"; and, after a held re-pin, "we need
to repin of course! i keep telling you not to block for these things."

## How to apply

- "Authority comes from the operator" is about where authority originates. It
  does not require the operator to retype an approval already given.
- Relay it in the handoff: quote the exact words, give the date, and name the
  specific action they cover. Then proceed.
- Ask again only for a genuinely new class of action: new production work,
  customer data, billing, a compatibility change.
- A later hold overrides an earlier go only for what it names, and only until
  it is lifted.
