The operator does not follow a lane's detail. Questions phrased in the lane's
own vocabulary stalled, and the operator said so repeatedly in October 2026:
"i do not know what a is. when you ask me for something i need the context and
the full questions"; "what are those? i am out of context have no idea what
your task names refer to. status in plain words? blockers?"; "i do not
understand what you are blocked on, explain and give me a recommendation both
of you agree on"; "do whaat makes sense and what is the correct solution, do
not ask me for decisions for which i do not have context". A from-scratch A/B
explanation of the same question then got an immediate ruling.

## How to apply

- Give the background from scratch, the options with what each one costs, one
  recommendation agreed with the peer seats first, and what is actually
  blocked. Use no task ids, phase codes or "option A".
- Status is plain words: what works, what does not, what is blocked and on whom.
- Ask only what needs the operator's context. Routine technical choices become
  recorded defaults the operator can judge at design review.
- When challenged, re-derive the answer from first principles against the code
  rather than repeating it from memory ("please think about this from first
  principles").

This sharpens the escalation rule in [drive coordination to done](../aweb-expert/lessons/drive-coordination-to-done.md):
that concept says which questions to escalate; this says how to ask them.
