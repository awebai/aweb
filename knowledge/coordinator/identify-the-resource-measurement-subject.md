title: Identify the resource measurement subject
description: A recovery threshold is meaningless when the measurement selects the wrong process or mixes counting units.
timestamp: 2026-10-01
---

Verify that discovery selects the actual resource holder. On September 30,
2026, a proposed Docker cleanup check selected a launcher with roughly forty
lsof rows while excluding the Apple Virtualization process with over fifty-five
thousand. A second system-wide counter did not repair the incorrect process
assertion. Archived process evidence corroborates the mismatch.

Retain candidate processes, command lines and evidence tying the selected process
to the relevant data path. Zero or multiple plausible subjects is an unavailable
measurement, not a passing sample. A changed process between samples cannot
establish same-process recovery. Test discovery with representative current
process lists rather than only a historical process name.

Keep units explicit. lsof rows include mappings and other non-descriptor
entries; numeric descriptor rows and kernel open-file structures are different
quantities. Preserve raw observations and compare like with like. Record stable
identities as well as counts so replacement does not masquerade as recovery.
